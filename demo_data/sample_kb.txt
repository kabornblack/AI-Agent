Wise is a global technology company that provides online money transfer and multi-currency accounts.
Users can send, spend, and receive money internationally with low fees and transparent exchange rates.

Transfers usually take between a few seconds and 2 business days depending on the route and currencies.
If a transfer is delayed, users can track its status in the Wise dashboard or mobile app.

Wise is regulated by financial authorities in all the countries where it operates.
If you experience an issue with your payment, contact Wise support or file a complaint from your account settings.

Refunds typically take 2–5 business days once processed.
